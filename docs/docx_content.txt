CVD Risk Predictor — AI-powered Heart Disease Detection System
Category
Web Technology, Real-Time Communication (WebRTC), and Human Resources (HR) Technology
Overview
R
CVD Risk Predictor is a web-based platform designed to assess the likelihood of cardiovascular disease using advanced machine learning techniques. The system utilizes a trained neural network model converted into ONNX format to deliver fast and efficient real-time predictions. It allows healthcare professionals and individuals to input key clinical parameters and receive accurate risk evaluations. The application features a modern React-based frontend integrated with a Node.js backend, ensuring a smooth, reliable, and scalable user experience. By analyzing 13 health indicators such as age, sex, chest pain type, blood pressure, cholesterol, ECG results, and more, the system generates a probability score and classifies patients into Low, Moderate, or High-risk categories, providing a comprehensive and intelligent approach to cardiovascular risk assessment.

Product
 Perspective
 
The CVD Risk Predictor is a web-based intelligent healthcare platform designed to assess an individual’s risk of cardiovascular disease using AI-powered algorithms. Unlike traditional risk calculators, it evaluates 13 clinical and lifestyle parameters to provide a more comprehensive and accurate assessment. The system is built with a React (Vite) frontend for a fast and responsive user interface, and a Node.js with Express.js backend that communicates with a neural network model deployed via ONNX Runtime for efficient, cross-platform inference. It operates entirely online without requiring installation and supports secure, real-time data processing with privacy-focused architecture

Conclusion
Conclusion
The CVD Risk Predictor system effectively integrates machine learning, web technologies, and healthcare data analysis to deliver an intelligent cardiovascular risk assessment platform. It combines 13 clinical and lifestyle parameters with an ONNX-based neural network model to generate fast, accurate, and reliable risk predictions in real time. The system enhances accessibility by providing a web-based interface that works across multiple devices without installation, ensuring wider reach and usability.
With its stateless and privacy-preserving architecture, the system ensures that no patient data is stored, thereby improving security and compliance with data protection standards. Features such as step-by-step data entry, animated risk visualization, and automated PDF report generation improve usability and make health insights easy to understand for users and healthcare professionals alike.
Overall, the CVD Risk Predictor contributes to preventive healthcare by enabling early detection of cardiovascular risk, improving awareness, and supporting timely medical consultation. Its scalable design and efficient AI integration make it a strong foundation for future enhancements such as advanced analytics, explainable AI, and integration with healthcare systems
.

Limitations
 
Limitations
The system provides only risk assessment and cannot replace professional medical diagnosis; users must consult healthcare professionals for confirmation and treatment
.
Prediction accuracy depends on the quality and diversity of the training dataset; performance may vary across different populations
.
The system relies entirely on user-provided input, and inaccurate or estimated data may lead to unreliable results
.
It does not consider additional clinical factors such as medical history, family history, genetic conditions, or ongoing medications
.
The model is static and does not update or learn from new data unless retrained and redeployed
.
A stable internet connection is required as the application is fully web-based
.
No user data is stored, so previous assessments cannot be retrieved without manual saving
.
Risk evaluation is limited to the 13 predefined parameters and excludes other potentially relevant health factors
.
The system does not integrate with Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems or external healthcare databases
.
Currently, the platform supports only the English language and lacks multilingual capabilities
.
